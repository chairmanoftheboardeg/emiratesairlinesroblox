Organizational Hierarchy

Leadership & Structure

A high-performing virtual airline requires a flawless organizational framework. Discover the elite departments, specialized divisions, and dedicated roles that work in unison to deliver the ultimate passenger experience across Emirates Group Roblox.

Tier 01

Executive Department

The highest echelon of leadership within Emirates Airlines. This department is responsible for setting the strategic vision, enforcing platform-wide Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), and managing the global trajectory of our network.

Chief Executive Officer

The highest-ranking officer in the organization. The CEO establishes the ultimate strategic vision, oversees all network operations, and ensures the highest standards of realism, community management, and corporate integrity are flawlessly maintained.

Deputy Chief Executive Officer

The second-in-command. The DCEO assists in daily operational management, policy enforcement, and inter-departmental coordination, ensuring the CEO's vision translates into seamless execution on the ground and in the air.

Tier 02

Flight Operations Department

The absolute core of our airline. Divided into two highly specialized sectors, the Flight Operations Department guarantees that every scheduled route is executed with unmatched precision, safety, and immersive realism.

Flight Deck Division

Tasked with the safe navigation and technical operation of our fleet.

Chief Pilot

The head of the Flight Deck Division. The Chief Pilot is responsible for aviator training standards, flight evaluation, SOP enforcement, and managing the active roster of pilots across the network.

Captain

The commander of the aircraft. The Captain holds ultimate responsibility for the safety, navigation, and highly professional execution of their scheduled PTFS/ROAV flights, making critical decisions from pushback to parking.

First Officer

The second-in-command of the aircraft. The First Officer works in seamless tandem with the Captain, managing communications, systems, and co-piloting duties to ensure a flawless journey.

Trainee Pilot

An aviator in training. Trainees undergo rigorous simulation and operational assessments to prove their proficiency and dedication before earning their wings to fly commercial routes.

Cabin Services Division

The face of Emirates Airlines, dedicated to world-class hospitality and passenger safety.

Purser

The highest-ranking cabin crew member on board. The Purser manages the entire in-flight service operation, coordinates the flight attendants, and ensures premium guest satisfaction and cabin security.

Flight Attendant

The dedicated frontline of our airline. Flight Attendants execute meticulous trolley services, manage passenger requests, and uphold the signature Emirates standard of comfort across all cabin classes.
