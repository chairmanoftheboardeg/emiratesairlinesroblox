Featured Destinations

Explore our primary PTFS network hubs.

IRFD

Greater Rockford

Rockford International

Greater Rockford Airport is a large international airport located on the island of Greater Rockford. It is one of the most popular and busiest airports in the game due to its central location and large size, making it a common starting place for many players.

IPPH

Perth

Perth International

Perth International is a large international airport and primary airport for the island of Perth that can handle both passenger and freighter operations.

ITKO

Orenji

Tokyo International

Tokyo International Airport is a large international airport located on the island of Orenji. Known for its challenging approaches and massive capacity.

ILAR

Cyprus

Larnaca International

Larnaca International Airport is one of the four airports on the island of Cyprus. It is the 3rd largest airport in the game, featuring unique ocean-bound runway approaches.
